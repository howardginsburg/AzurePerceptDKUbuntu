Device Settings

LOCAL OUTPUT

HDMI display

Console or camera

Console is the text login on the monitor, as before. Camera plays the Eye's video full screen, with no desktop. Everything else keeps running in both modes.

In camera mode, Ctrl+Alt+F1 on a USB keyboard shows the console and Ctrl+Alt+F7 the camera again. If the video can't be shown, the console comes back and the reason appears above.

EYE

Vision & camera

Models

Upload a model under Models & files, then pick it in Model. An XML model needs its BIN file beside it. The model must suit the Myriad X (OpenVINO); see eye.md for the formats and parsers.

The video stops for up to a minute while the Eye loads a model. If a model doesn't load, the Eye uses the default SSD instead, unless Strict model is 1. Its log is under System.

EAR & MOUTH

Audio & Bluetooth

Bluetooth speakers

Put the speaker in pairing mode, scan, then Connect. Pairing takes up to a minute.

VOICE SAMPLE

Voice commands

INFERENCES

MQTT

The Eye and the voice sample share these. Leave the URL empty for the device's own broker. Upload password and certificate files under Models & files.

GRAFANA & PROMETHEUS

Monitoring

How much metrics history Prometheus keeps. The eMMC is small: leave room for image updates.

CONFIG/FILES

Models & files

Upload

Models, label files, broker passwords and certificates. The containers see them as /config/files/<name>. A file with the same name is replaced.
